You are making hero art for the emails of Token Circles, an open-source personal finance app (web, local-first or cloud). The picture sits at the top of the welcome email, the confirm-your-email email and a "finance print" email. The name is the brand: circles of money that come round every month and every year, the people in one household, a ring that fills as a goal gets closer. These are design variants so the owner can choose a direction. They are not final assets.

House style for every image:

- Landscape 3:2, 1536x1024. It is shown 600 px wide in an email and as small as 360 px wide on phones, so it needs one clear subject with a strong silhouette that still reads when small.
- Composition: the subject sits right of centre and may be cropped by the right edge. The left 40 percent of the frame is plain, empty ground with a soft falloff, so live text can sit beside it. Keep a safe margin of about 8 percent at the top and bottom.
- Palette: deep ink navy #0a0e1c and #131c39, steel blue #3b6fe0 and #6e9bff, paper white #f7f9ff, and one small warm copper accent (#f0a860 or #d97f2e) used sparingly. The light variants use paper white as the ground and ink blue as the main colour.
- Finish: crafted and physical. It must look like a real object, a real print or a real photograph made by a skilled person: engraving, paper, metal, stone. It must not look like digital glow art.
- Never: neon, bloom haze, lens flares, glowing blobs, gradient meshes, glass panels, sparkles, stars, planets, space scenes, rockets, plastic 3D-render surfaces, people, hands, faces, portraits, animals.
- Absolutely no text anywhere: no letters, numbers, digits, currency symbols, words, logos, watermarks, signatures, monograms, hallmarks, mint marks, serial numbers, denominations, inscriptions, captions or interface elements. No real banknotes or coins of any country, no real brands. Any coin or token is a smooth blank disc with no markings at all.

Image 1, file a1-engraved-night.png. "Engraved orbits, night plate." A macro photograph of an intaglio print, the fine engraving craft used on banknotes and watch dials. Its subject is one large guilloche rosette made of hundreds of hair-thin interlaced orbit lines that together form a ring with twelve soft lobes, like twelve months going round. It is printed in pale steel blue with a few lines in copper, on a deep ink-navy plate, right of centre and partly cropped by the right edge. A few fine concentric orbit lines continue faintly across the plate around it. Raking light shows the raised ink and the texture of the cotton paper. The left 40 percent is plain navy with only the paper texture. No numerals, no lettering, no portrait, no seal: only lines.
